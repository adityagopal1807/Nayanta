FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

We deliver pan-India in 3–5 business days. Orders above ₹999 get free shipping. You'll receive a tracking link once your order is shipped.

Can I return or exchange my glasses?

Yes! We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy. If the frames don't fit or you're not satisfied, contact us and we'll arrange a return or exchange at no extra cost.

Are the products genuine?

Absolutely. We source all products directly from verified Lenskart-authorised channels. Every frame comes with original packaging and quality assurance.

Do you offer prescription glasses?

Yes! Our "Powered" range lets you order prescription lenses starting at ₹999. Contact us on WhatsApp with your prescription details and we'll guide you through the process.

How does the Virtual Try-On work?

Our Virtual Try-On uses your webcam or an uploaded photo. It detects your face and overlays any frame from our collection so you can see exactly how it looks before buying — no download required.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, wallets, and WhatsApp Pay. Cash on Delivery is available in select areas — contact us to confirm your PIN code.
